# Diversity and Ensemble Learning in Fraud Detection

- **Source:** Paldino et al., 2022/2024, The Role of Diversity and Ensemble
  Learning in Credit Card Fraud Detection.
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11634-022-00515-5
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9516537/
- **Method:** Diversity-aware ensembles over large transaction streams.
- **Finding:** Complementary errors can improve fixed-budget precision, though
  gains vary across streams.
- **DAC relevance:** Justifies the compact ensemble screens already run in V2
  and V3.
- **Caveat:** Existing DAC seed, family, and boundary blends did not produce a
  stable gain of the required size.
