# Delayed Feedback Fraud Detection

- **Source:** Dal Pozzolo et al., 2018, Credit Card Fraud Detection: A
  Realistic Modeling and a Novel Learning Strategy.
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/TNNLS.2017.2736643
- **Method:** Delayed feedback modeling and drift-aware evaluation.
- **Finding:** Temporal feedback handling matters for realistic fraud ranking.
- **DAC relevance:** A possible future lever if trusted claim timestamps or
  post-decision feedback become available.
- **Caveat:** The Worldline/card stream and temporal protocol are different from
  the current static DAC table.
